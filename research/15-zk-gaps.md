## Conflicts noticed

- Images may be embedded in notes via Markdown, kept in a media sub-folder (help page, #how-can-i-include-images-in-my-notes) vs embedded images are not used in Zettelkasten notes (03-221).
- Saved search paired with an index note (release notes v0.4.2) vs saved searches don't create or manifest structure (05-526). Tension, not strict conflict.
- New tags: other:images (images embedded in notes), other:template (note header/template).
- Note body is "generally a short, atomic piece of information" (zh /introduction/zh/#单个笔记卡片的构成, Sascha Fast tr. Zhixiang Cai; wording absent from current English) vs. atomicity is not shortness and idea atoms come in different sizes (02-097, 02-124).
- Structure notes "do not contain any new content on their own" (Demo note 201705180836 Structure notes, Sascha Fast and Christian Tietze) vs. structure note opening with a bolded concept definition/summary and a visualization (02-127, 02-158; core image claim on slow-twitch-gap.webp) and writing text into structure notes (04-208).
- A structure note does "not simply list all the notes that are important for some purpose" (Demo note 201705180836) vs. link every new Zettel on a topic from that topic's Structure Note (02-051).
- Luhmann's register "is not important" for a digital archive because full-text and tag search give entry (Demo note 201705111034 Luhmanns Zettelkasten) vs. limit entry into the hypertext to a few central entry points, register-style (02-009).
- New tag: other:visualization (slow-twitch-gap.webp old-figure claim; Demo note 201705221802 on drawing/images).
