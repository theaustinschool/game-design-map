## Conflicts noticed

- Atomicity versus narrative. 14-003 and 14-017 favor atomic or redundant small cards. 14-010 says stories are rarely atomic but worth violating atomicity for. 14-004 re-adds a combined card to integrate atomic ones. Compare 10-011, 10-018, 10-019, 10-020, 10-054.
- Who writes the prompts. 14-007, 14-043, 14-044, and 14-046 say writing your own prompts is itself understanding. 14-008 says expert-written prompts may compensate. 14-042 names the trade-off. Compare 10-002 and 10-042 on own words.
- Highlighting. 14-034 says highlighting mostly does not help internalize material. 10-078 recommends highlighting or noting key details for hard texts. 14-021 and 14-036 build on highlights as the capture primitive.
- Reading-note form. 14-057 calls a separate precis too decontextualized. 10-039 summarizes a book in its own terms in a separate note about the book. 14-056 calls marginalia too subordinate to the text, and 10-082 says margins make it hard to see across texts.
- Unit size. 14-066 favors cards that hold "a real chunk" for synthesis. 10-011 and 10-047 favor very small notes.
- Note-writing as work versus displacement. 14-026 calls writing up notes a possible displacement behavior. 10-022 and 10-080 treat note-writing as the core daily thinking practice.
- Handwriting versus files. 14-029, 14-032, 14-061, and 14-064 describe handwritten, offline work. 10-035 says notes should be a folder of text files not trapped in a system.
