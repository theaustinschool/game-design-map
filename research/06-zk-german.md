## Conflicts noticed

- Atomicity as rule vs. reasoned principle. 06-044 says atomicity is not a rule followed on principle but a principle followed for good reasons. 06-180 says the cardinality metric requires following atomicity so that one zettel equals one thought.
- Excerpt first vs. no excerpts. 06-128 and 06-126 describe writing a large excerpt outside the Zettelkasten and zetteling it later. 06-201 (Luhmann, as quoted) says reading for zetteling means "not: excerpts!". 06-127 and 06-078 say books are normally processed directly into zettels, with work starting only once thoughts leave the source.
- Raw vs. finished notes. 06-197 and 06-205 (Luhmann, as quoted) say not to put only clarified notes in and that arbitrary ideas may enter. 06-028 and 06-209 require each note to be understandable without context in 5 to 10 years and value-creating at the moment of writing.
- Keeping sources. 06-131 says Sascha keeps no sources after processing. 06-156 describes a feeder system that keeps links to PDFs and websites.
- Software. 06-150 says software power is unimportant and pen and paper suffice. 06-026 and 06-030 set hard requirements for speed and for scale to tens of thousands of notes.
- Search. 06-091 says search becomes unreliable at scale. 06-137 describes using a saved search to reach the favorites' zettels when placing a new zettel.
- DE/EN divergence (not a conflict between claims). The German iceberg post contains the Luhmann passage behind 06-033 ("every zettel needs at least one link"), and the English version has no such passage. See the coverage log.
