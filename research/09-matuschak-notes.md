# Matuschak: working notes crawl

Ticket: https://github.com/theaustinschool/game-design-map/issues/9
Scope: Andy Matuschak's public working notes (notes.andymatuschak.org), breadth-first over links and backlinks from "Evergreen notes", limited to notes about note-making.
Swept: 2026-11-04

Totals: 201 claims; 63 read, 6 read-no-claims, 111 skipped, 0 failed.

### Access method

The site is a single-page app, but each note URL returns server-rendered HTML that embeds a `<script id="notetower-initial-data" type="application/json">` block. Its `noteCache[<id>].data` holds `title`, `contentMarkdown`, `linkedNoteSlugs`, and `backlinkedNotes` (with the linking paragraph as context). The crawl fetched `https://notes.andymatuschak.org/<id>` with a plain HTTP GET (Python `urllib`, no JavaScript) and parsed that block. Slug URLs such as `/Evergreen_notes` redirect to the canonical `/<id>` URL. Two other first-party paths exist and were checked: `/notes/manifest.json` (title-to-id map of 5,856 public notes) and `/notes/<id>.md` (raw Markdown body plus an appended `## Backlinks` section, with the same body text as `contentMarkdown`). Every quote below was checked as an exact substring of the fetched `contentMarkdown`, after rendering `[[id:::Title]]` links as their title text and stripping Markdown emphasis.

### Crawl boundary

BFS started at "Evergreen notes" (`z5E5QawiXCMbtNtupvxeoEX`). Each reached note got a hand decision, `on` or `off`, recorded before it was expanded. Only `on` notes were fetched and expanded through both outgoing links and backlinks. The BFS converged when no reached note was undecided.

On-topic means the note's subject is note-making itself: what a good note is, atomicity, concept orientation, linking and link context, titles, tags and indexes, note types, literature notes, inboxes and capture feeding notes, reading-to-notes processes, note maintenance and review, note-writing practice and its failure modes, and note-writing systems as they bear on these.

Off-topic means the subject is something else, even when reached from a note-making note: spaced repetition and the mnemonic medium, general learning and memory research, general reading advice, general writing and creativity, knowledge work in general, enabling environments, collaboration and conversation, interface and software design, individual software products, people, books and papers (except the Ahrens literature note, which is about note-making), and site meta or personal logs. Decisions were made from the note title and the linking context, so skipped notes were not fetched.
