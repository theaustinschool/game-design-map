## Conflicts noticed

- Folgezettel: essential to the method (Lüdecke, as summarized, 02-166, 02-168) vs. one technique among many, with links giving richer context (Sascha, 02-167, 02-169).
- Note size: "limit each Zettel to one thought" and "shortest writing session that could possibly be useful" (02-030, 02-093) vs. atomicity is not shortness, atoms come in different sizes, and a problem-solution pair or a concept-turned-model can stay on one note (02-097, 02-119, 02-121, 02-124).
- Atomicity as entry condition vs. goal: "limit its content to that single topic" (02-073) vs. "goal to achieve, instead of the condition a note has to meet" and "Ignore every rule when you enter a new idea" (02-135, 02-138).
- Unfinished notes: half-finished notes belong in the Zettelkasten, with no deadline to mature (02-130, 02-131, 02-132) vs. "A note should be fully developed, not just scribbles" (02-207) and notes whose value is timeless (02-163).
- Tags: Luhmann's register was entry points only and his notes were not tagged (02-006, 02-007) vs. "Don't use categories. Use tags instead." and "object tags (highly recommended)" (02-069, 02-145).
- Hierarchy: Luhmann rejects topical order and privileged notes (02-171, 02-182) vs. "Some sort of hierarchical structure is very useful" and Main Structure Notes as a top layer (02-048, 02-083).
- Verbatim quotes: "nothing wrong with capturing a verbatim quote on top" (02-027) vs. Luhmann: quotes "only worth collecting when they are exceptionally concise" (02-187).
- What to admit: "If in doubt, write the note" (02-034) vs. "Don't put everything and anything in your Zettelkasten" and tier D/F ideas stay out (02-150, 02-149).
- Wikis: "a wiki is a type of software that you could use (though it's not recommended)" (02-192) vs. DokuWiki as "one of my favourite software solutions", given one thought per linked subsection (02-060).

New `other:` tags used in this file, for synthesis to consider promoting: `other:body`, `other:knowledge` (knowledge vs. information), `other:scope` (what belongs in the Zettelkasten), `other:form` (inner vs. outer form), `other:layers` (layers of evidence), `other:output` (writing from notes), `other:holistic`, `other:summary` (one-sentence summary), `other:maturity` (note maturation stages), `other:purpose`, `other:backlinks`.
